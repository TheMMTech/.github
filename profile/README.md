## How I work

- **On your side.** I work for you. My job is the right answer for your business, whoever ends up doing the work.
- **Advice, not a help desk.** Your IT provider does the hands-on work. I make sure it is the right provider and that
  the work gets done.
- **AI pilots, then handed off.** The one thing I build: an AI pilot on your own data, tested and hardened, then handed
  to your IT provider or the vendor to run while I oversee it. I never run production myself.
- **Plain English.** You should understand every recommendation well enough to explain it to your own team.
- **Platform-neutral.** Google Workspace and Microsoft 365; Azure, Google Cloud and AWS.
